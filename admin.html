A LITTLE FORESIGHT GOES A LONG WAY

Inventory

Restock when stock reaches its reorder point. Suggestions use completed sales, supplier lead time, and your safety stock.

How to read this table

Product / supplier: The item and its supplier.

On hand: Units currently in stock.

Daily / weekly forecast: Estimated units sold per day and week, averaged from completed weeks.

Lead / safety: Supplier delivery time in days, followed by extra units held as a buffer.

ROP (reorder point): Daily forecast × lead days + safety units, rounded up. Reorder when stock reaches or drops below this point.

Status: Healthy means stock is above warning levels; Low means it has reached a warning level; Reorder means stock is at or below ROP. No sales means there is not enough history to forecast.

Suggested qty: Units needed to reach target stock, covering lead time, 14 extra days of demand, and safety stock. A dash means no reorder is suggested.

Restock: Add received units and record the change. Parameters: Edit the product and its stock planning settings.
